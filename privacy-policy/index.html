Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Human Anatomy application by XR View Labs for Meta Quest.

Hand tracking

Human Anatomy uses hand-tracking input provided by your Meta Quest headset so you can interact with the experience. The app processes this input on your headset while you use it. XR View Labs does not receive or retain hand-tracking data, and the app does not transmit it.

Data collection and sharing

Human Anatomy does not collect, store, or share personal data. The app does not require an XR View Labs account and does not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services. Meta’s handling of data through the Meta Quest platform is described in Meta’s privacy policy.

Support email

If you email XR View Labs, we use your email address and the information you provide to respond to your request.

Data deletion requests and privacy contact

XR View Labs holds no personal data from your use of the app. For privacy questions or to request deletion of information you shared with us by email, contact xrviewlabs@gmail.com.
